Common questions

How are waitlist openings calculated?

The planner subtracts confirmed seats from capacity and limits new offers to the number on your waitlist. Count active unexpired offers as confirmed while planning so you never allocate a seat twice.

How should I estimate no-shows?

Use comparable workshops and start with the number still confirmed at your cutoff. Subtract confirmed guests who checked in, then divide by the number confirmed at the cutoff. Treat the example percentage as a scenario if you have no prior data.

Can I keep my current registration tool?

Yes. Use the planner alongside your current sign-up form. Keep attendee contact lists in your registration tool. The planner only needs totals.

What data does this planner use?

It uses aggregate counts in this tab. Calculations and downloads run on your device. This page sets no cookies, adds no analytics scripts and asks for no names, emails or payment details. A hosting provider may process requests under its own terms.

What are the planner's limitations?

Results are estimates. Confirm the venue's actual usable capacity and count staff or reserved seats where relevant. A forecast of empty seats never increases the number of safe offers.
