# SeatRelay Attendance Planner

A free, offline tool for planning attendance at workshops and other small events.

1. [Download SeatRelay-Planner.html](https://github.com/fdijo-cyber/seatrelay/releases/download/planner-v1.0.0/SeatRelay-Planner.html).
2. Open the downloaded file in a browser.
3. Enter your venue capacity, confirmed bookings, waitlist count, and expected no-show rate.

The planner estimates turnout, caps waitlist offers at the number of unreserved seats, and exports your plan as a CSV. It also includes a reconfirmation checklist with wording for the invite, waitlist offer, and final reminder.

For a workshop with 40 seats, 36 confirmations, 12 people on the waitlist, and an estimated no-show rate of 20%, it forecasts 29 arrivals and four seats you can safely offer.

Use counts only. The planner runs in your browser and needs no account or internet connection once downloaded.
